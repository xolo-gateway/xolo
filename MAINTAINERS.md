## Becoming a committer or maintainer

The criteria are public and listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md);
committers and maintainers are named by the lead maintainer based on a
track record of merged contributions and review participation. Working for
the same employer as the lead maintainer, or funding the project, confers
no role by itself.
