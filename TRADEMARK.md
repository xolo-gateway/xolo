# Trademark policy

This document explains how the Xolo name and logo may be used. A French
translation is available in [`docs/fr/projet/marque.md`](docs/fr/projet/marque.md);
the English version is authoritative.

The AGPL-3.0 gives everyone the right to use, modify and redistribute the
software. It does not give anyone the right to use the name and logo of the
project in a way that suggests their work is the official one. This policy
draws that line so that a user who sees "Xolo" knows what they are getting.

## The marks

The word mark **Xolo** and the Xolo logo (the files under
`internal/http/handler/webui/common/assets/`: `logo.svg`, `banner.svg`,
`favicon.svg` and their bitmap variants) are registered in France with the
INPI, under filing numbers 5295297 for the word mark and 5295272 for the
logo. They are held by William Petit, the lead maintainer, until an
association is created to carry the project, at which point the marks will
be transferred to it. This policy will be updated on that day; the permissions
below will carry over unchanged.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to the project.** "Built on Xolo", "compatible with Xolo",
  "a plugin for Xolo", "hosted Xolo instance", "Xolo consulting": naming the
  software you work with is nominative use and is always allowed.
- **Redistribute the official releases** unchanged, under the Xolo name,
  including through a package or a container image, as long as you say
  which official version it is.
- **Host an official, unmodified version** for your users and call it Xolo.
- **Write plugins** and describe them as Xolo plugins, under any license
  (see [LICENSE-EXCEPTION](LICENSE-EXCEPTION)). A plugin may carry "Xolo" in
  its name in a descriptive position, for example `xolo-plugin-ldap` or
  "LDAP sync for Xolo".
- **Use the logo** to link to the project or to illustrate a talk, an
  article or a course about Xolo, unaltered and with no implication of
  endorsement.
- **Fork the code**, keep the repository name if that is how your hosting
  platform works, and mention that it is a fork of Xolo.

## What requires permission

- **Distributing a modified version under the Xolo name.** A fork that
  changes the code, or a build with non-official patches, must be shipped
  under another name, or under a name that makes the difference obvious, such
  as "Acme's fork of Xolo". A configuration change, a theme or a plugin does
  not make a version "modified" for this purpose; a patch to the core does.
- **Naming a product, a service, a company or a domain name** with "Xolo"
  in a way that could be taken for the project itself or an official
  offering: "Xolo Cloud", "Xolo Enterprise", `xolo-gateway.example`.
- **Suggesting endorsement or affiliation**: "official", "certified",
  "partner", or the logo on a page that sells a service, unless the project
  has said so.
- **Altering the logo**: changing its colors, proportions or elements,
  combining it with another mark.

Ask by opening an issue or by writing to the address listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Requests are answered in the open when the
request is public. Permission, when given, is non-exclusive and may be
withdrawn if the conditions under which it was granted no longer hold.

## Compatibility claims

You may say that your software works with Xolo, or with a given Xolo
version, when it does. Say which version, and do not present a compatibility
claim as a certification by the project.

## Reporting misuse

If you see the name or logo used in a way that misleads users about what is
official, tell us through the contact in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be relaxed at any time. Making it stricter follows the RFC
process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), with the thirty-day comment
period, and never applies retroactively to uses that were allowed when they
started.
